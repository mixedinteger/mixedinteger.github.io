About

The Mixed Integer Programming (MIP) Workshop is a single-track workshop highlighting the latest trends in integer programming and discrete optimization, with speakers chosen by invitation.

The 2027 edition of the workshop will be the twenty-fourth in the MIP series. Links to past editions can be found here. MIP 2027 is supported by the Mixed Integer Programming Society (MIPS), which is a section of the Mathematical Optimization Society (MOS).

The MIP 2027 organizing committee and the local organizing committee are committed to making the workshop a welcoming environment for all members of the MIP community. MIP 2027 will be an entirely in-person event. We look forward to seeing the community at the University of Iowa.
