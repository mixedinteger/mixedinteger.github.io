Registration

Registration is required to participate in the competition. Register your team with the registration form by January 22, 2027 (AoE). The form also asks you to disclose any conflict of interest, for example if you are related to a competition organizer (see the rules).

Please read the topic and the rules before registering. Details that are still to be announced are listed on the Rules page under To Be Announced; see also the timeline.
