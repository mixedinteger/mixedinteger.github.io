## Registration

Registration is required to participate in the competition. Register your team with the
[registration form](https://forms.gle/tQTnzVApHgwRw33X6) by **January 22, 2027 (AoE)**. The form also asks you to disclose any
conflict of interest, for example if you are related to a competition organizer (see the
[rules](rules.html#rules-for-participation)).

Please read the [topic](topic.html) and the [rules](rules.html) before registering. Details that
are still to be announced are listed on the Rules page under
[To Be Announced](rules.html#to-be-announced); see also the [timeline](index.html#timeline).
