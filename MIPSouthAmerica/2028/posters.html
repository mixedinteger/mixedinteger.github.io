Posters and Flash Talks

The MIP Workshop South America welcomes contributions in the following two formats:

Student posters

The workshop will include a poster session for presentations of undergraduate and graduate students. The poster presentations will be preceded by a special session in which the poster presenters will give two-minute “teaser talks” on their posters. There will be a limited number of available slots for posters, and the program committee will evaluate the submissions based on their merits. Selected poster presenters will be eligible for partial travel support, and the best poster will be recognized at the event.

Flash talks

Non-student researchers, academic and industry, are also invited to give a “flash talk” (i.e., a short talk) at the workshop. There will be a limited number of slots for flash talks, and the program committee will evaluate submissions on their merits. Flash talk presenters will not be eligible for travel support from the workshop organization.

Abstract Submission

Submissions for posters and flash talks are not yet open. We plan to open submissions in April 2027.

In both cases, a two-page abstract must be submitted, listing all authors and clearly indicating the presenting author. No particular text formatting is required for the abstracts, but PDF file format is mandatory.

All abstracts submitted before October 10, 2027 will receive a notification of acceptance/rejection by November 1, 2027.

The final deadline for abstract submission is November 30, 2027, and these submissions will receive a notification of acceptance/rejection by December 20, 2027.
