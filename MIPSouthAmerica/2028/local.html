Venue

MIP South America 2028 will be held at Universidad Torcuato Di Tella in Buenos Aires, Argentina. Campus Di Tella is located in the Belgrano neighborhood, at the intersection of Avenida Figueroa Alcorta and J. P. Sáenz Valiente. The campus brings together the university’s teaching and research activities in a leafy urban setting.

Address: Av. Figueroa Alcorta 7350, Buenos Aires, Argentina.

See the university’s directions to Campus Di Tella for information on reaching the venue.
