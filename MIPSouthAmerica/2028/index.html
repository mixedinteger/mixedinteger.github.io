About

The second Mixed Integer Programming Workshop South America will take place on March 13–16, 2028, at Universidad Torcuato Di Tella in Buenos Aires, Argentina.

The Mixed Integer Programming (MIP) Workshop is a single-track workshop highlighting the latest trends in integer programming and discrete optimization, with speakers chosen by invitation. It brings together researchers, students, and industry participants from around the world.

MIP South America is part of the MIP International Series of the Mixed Integer Programming Society, held in addition to the traditional annual MIP Workshop.
